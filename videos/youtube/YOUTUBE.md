Konglish: English words Koreans use in their own way! 📱 A cell phone is a "hand phone"!

• you can do it! = 화이팅 (hwa-i-ting) – literally "fighting!"
• bottoms up! = 원샷 (won-syat) – literally "one shot"
• comedian = 개그맨 (gae-geu-maen) – literally "gag man"
• convertible = 오픈카 (o-peun-ka) – literally "open car"
• soda / Sprite = 사이다 (sa-i-da) – literally "cider"
• free extra = 서비스 (seo-bi-seu) – literally "service"
• part-time job = 아르바이트 (a-reu-ba-i-teu) – literally "Arbeit (German!)"
• hugs & cuddles = 스킨십 (seu-kin-sip) – literally "skinship"
